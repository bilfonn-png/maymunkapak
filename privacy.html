MaymunKapak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

MaymunKapak (the website and the Chrome extension) makes YouTube thumbnails entirely in your browser.

  • Photos and titles you enter are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. We do not run servers that receive them.
  • YouTube channel mode (optional). If you connect your channel, Google gives your browser a temporary access token. It is used only to list your recent videos and, when you click "Upload to my channel", to set that video's thumbnail through the YouTube Data API. The token is kept in memory and is not stored or sent anywhere else. MaymunKapak's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Pro license. When you activate Pro, your license key is sent to our payment provider (Gumroad) to check it, and it is stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Settings such as your language are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking cookies.

Questions: contact the developer through the support email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
